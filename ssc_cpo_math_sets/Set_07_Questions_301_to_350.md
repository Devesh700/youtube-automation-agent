**Key Concept:** Commercial Mathematics: Cost Price, Selling Price & Successive Discounts

**Explanation:**
1. Let base Cost Price (CP) or Marked Price (MP) be 100 or as given in the problem.
2. Apply the successive discount formula: Net Discount = D1 + D2 - (D1 × D2)/100, or SP = CP × (100 ± Gain%/Loss%)/100.
3. Equate the effective selling price to the transaction amount.
4. Calculating the required value gives 25%.
